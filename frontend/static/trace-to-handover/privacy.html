Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Summary

Trace-to-Handover runs entirely on Atlassian Forge ("Runs on Atlassian"). It sends no data to any server outside Atlassian, uses no third-party services and no analytics.

Data the app processes

  • Confluence page content you choose to import, to extract requirement statements.
  • Jira work item summaries, descriptions, statuses and labels in the project you open, to build the traceability matrix.

Data the app stores

  • In Forge storage, per Jira project: the extracted requirement list, the source page ID/title/version, an import change log (timestamps and requirement IDs), and a link to the last generated report. No user identifiers or other personal data are stored.
  • In your Jira site: labels of the form trace-REQ-01 on work items you link.
  • In your Confluence site: acceptance report pages and, if you use demo data, a sample Terms of Reference page.

All requests use the permissions of the signed-in user. Data stays in your Atlassian site and Atlassian's Forge platform, subject to Atlassian's privacy policy.

Deletion

Uninstalling the app removes its Forge storage. Labels and pages created in Jira and Confluence remain your content and can be removed by you.
